# Prospects: Texas aerobic maintenance providers worth calling first

Compiled October 12, 2026, from public sources only: county OSSF pages and their published provider lists, TCEQ's occupational-licence search (which lists licensed individuals by county of their licence address, not companies), and the companies' own websites and public directory listings. Nobody has been contacted. Business listings only; the only names are owners or licensees as a county list or the company's own site publishes them.

How to read the columns:
- **Size**: what the public record suggests. "3 licensed MPs on the county list" is observed; "likely 200 to 1,000 contracts" is a guess and is marked as one. Nothing public states contract counts.
- **Software**: what was actually seen on the website. Across about 40 sites in the three regions, **not one showed a Jobber client hub, a ServiceTitan portal or a Fieldbase link**. The visible software is bill-pay pages, Housecall Pro at two companies, Square, PayPal, Calendly and Venmo. Most are phone and a contact form.
- **Call order**: 1 is first. Hays, Williamson and the LCRA lake counties come first because those are the authorities whose rules are deepest in the product; the Bell, Grayson and SJRA rows are for a second wave.

Add the three answers from each call (software today, what they hate about filing, what would make them switch) in the Notes column after the call, with the date.

## What the research turned up about the counties themselves

These are observations from the agents' reading of county pages on October 12, 2026. None is confirmed with the county; each is a thing to check against the matching profile in `docs/jurisdictions/` before the first call in that county.

- **Which counties publish a provider list.** Hays (2024 PDF, maintenance providers on p. 3), Guadalupe (June 2026 PDF), Grayson (March 2026 scan, individuals only), SJRA (undated PDF with company names). **Not** Williamson (the homeowner packet the search engines index as the list now returns 404), Comal (installers and evaluators only), Caldwell, Travis, Burnet, Llano, Bastrop, Bell or Montgomery; those point to the TCEQ search.
- **TCEQ's search** lists individuals by the county of their licence address and gives no company name, so a Lake Travis provider licensed out of a Williamson address does not appear under Travis. Counts found: Hays 32, Comal 34, Caldwell 12, Williamson 23, Travis 22, Burnet 4, Llano 5, Bastrop 25, Bell 21, Montgomery 50.
- **Comal County has a published upload channel for providers**: two Files.com drops, one for maintenance contracts and one for maintenance reports, signed in with name, email and TCEQ licence number (linked from comalcounty.gov/274). Already in the Comal profile (`tx-comal-county.md`, filed by hand on the portal); the research corroborates it.
- **LCRA** says providers email the contract or report with a cover sheet to OSSF@LCRA.ORG; the cover sheet is a fillable PDF with contract dates, inspections per year and the report date. Already in the LCRA profile (`tx-lcra-ossf.md`): Aerowise fills LCRA's own cover sheet, pinned by hash. Still open there: whether LCRA accepts it filled electronically.
- **SJRA** has a customer portal for submitting contracts and reports (customerportal.sjra.net). Already in the SJRA profile (`tx-sjra-ossf.md`, entered by hand on the portal).
- **Grayson** expects a per-contract filing fee ("verify filing fee is included or remit fee directly to Grayson County with a copy of the contract") and its testing-and-reporting form still says to mail it, while permits run through MyGovernmentOnline.
- **Bell County** lists an email for "maintenance contract tracking information" (bcphdtracking@bellcountyhealth.org) on its contact page; whether reports go there is not stated.
- **Travis County** Chapter 448 lists report contents beyond 30 TAC 285 (unauthorized alterations, spray-area condition, permit number, provider addresses and phones) but no submission channel; permits run through MyPermitNow.
- **Williamson**: the current OSSF pages offer a homeowner self-maintenance packet and a "guide to evaluating service contracts"; the provider submission channel was not readable in the pane. The profile's question 1 (which inbox) stands.
- **Hays**: nothing new; no provider channel is published, as the profile says.
- Provider copy across the three regions says "we file with the county for you" without naming a tool. The only field software seen was Housecall Pro (Modern Septic, Waco Septic). Nobody mentioned Fieldbase.
