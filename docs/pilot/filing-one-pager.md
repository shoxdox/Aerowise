# "How do I file my four-month reports in my county with this?"

Written October 12, 2026, from the two county profiles in `docs/jurisdictions/` (`tx-hays-county.md` v2, `tx-williamson-county.md` v2) and the rulesets in `packages/compliance`. Both profiles are **drafts built from the counties' public documents; neither county has confirmed anything**. Every line marked *unconfirmed* is Aerowise's reading, and the pilot is where it gets checked. Give the provider the page for their county; keep the "Status" line in.

---

## Hays County (Development Services, OSSF)

**Status:** draft from public documents. Hays County has published no way for providers to submit reports, so today Aerowise prepares every Hays report and you send it the way you do now.

**What the county wants** (county OSSF order, Section 10.V; 30 TAC 285.7):
- A test and report every four months unless the permit says otherwise. The first within 14 days of the contract start; later ones counted from the start date; the last within 14 days of the contract end.
- The report to the county and a copy to the homeowner, within 14 days of the test.
- If a report shows low chlorine or anything not working: fix it, retest, and send another report within 10 days of the previous one. That extra report does not move the regular schedule.
- A new contract at least 30 days before service stops; on a property sale, a signed contract to Development Services within 30 days.
- A renewal with the same provider is not accepted until every report from the previous period is in. A contract with a new provider runs at least a year with four inspections.
- Every contract shows the permit number, your licence, both parties' printed names and signatures, start and end dates, the system's address and phone, and your physical address, business address, business phone and emergency phone.
- Providers register with Development Services every year (free, expires December 31).

**How Aerowise prepares it:**
- When you enter a contract, it books the visits on the county's count: a 12-month contract gets four, dated from the start, the last in the final two weeks. If your plan only has three, it asks before adding the fourth.
- The technician does the inspection on the phone (works with no signal), with photos and chlorine reading; the report is generated with your licence number and the county's required fields and held for you to look at.
- The inbox shows each report's due date (14 days after the test, or the visit's own due date if that comes first) and warns five days before.
- A zero chlorine reading, a chlorine device needing service, or any failed item puts "Fix, retest and report again" in the inbox, due 10 days later, and books the retest as an extra visit without touching the contract's four.
- A same-provider renewal is held while a previous-period report is unsent; a short contract that might follow another provider's is held with the reason.
- If the permit sets a different interval (six months, say), you record it on the site with where the permit says so, and the schedule follows the permit.

**What you would still do by hand today:**
- **Send every report and every contract yourself**, the way you do now (the county has published no email, portal or upload for providers; MyGovernmentOnline is named for permit applications only). Aerowise marks the filing as sent when you tell it you did, and keeps the PDF.
- Confirm each new site is in unincorporated Hays County, not in a city that runs its own OSSF program (the county does not list them).
- Keep your own record of the annual county registration; Aerowise does not track it yet.

**Drafts not confirmed with the county** (each is a question for the county, not a guess we are hiding):
- *Unconfirmed:* where reports and contracts go. ossfmaintenance@hayscountytx.gov is the county's listed inquiry address; it is **not** used for filing until the county says it takes reports.
- *Unconfirmed:* "within 14 days of the end of the contract period" read as the 14 days **before** the end. The warning on the fourth visit says so.
- *Unconfirmed:* each visit's report due by that visit's due date when that is sooner than 14 days after the test.
- *Unconfirmed:* the 10-day corrective clock counted from the day the previous report was sent, and "required chlorine residual" read as any zero reading.
- *Unconfirmed:* which cities run their own program; what triggers the $50 contract late fee; the registration form.

**What the pilot proves:** one real report, prepared here, compared line by line with one the county already accepted, then sent by you. If the county answers the "where do reports go" question, a new ruleset version can send them.

## Williamson County (Engineer's Office / Department of Infrastructure, OSSF)

**Status:** draft from public documents. Williamson publishes an email address, so Aerowise can send the report by email, but only after you press Send on each one. Nothing goes to the county on its own.

**What the county wants** (county OSSF order 2021, Section 10; the Routine Maintenance Affidavit; the homeowner maintenance packet):
- A test and report every four months; every six months for a system with electronic monitoring on record.
- Each report to include the sludge level in the pump tank and the condition of the spray area (order Section 10.E.7(c)).
- The report filed within 10 days of the test (the affidavit; the homeowner packet and TCEQ say 14; Aerowise uses 10, the strictest).
- Renewed contracts filed with the county 30 days before the previous one expires, or within 30 days after a property transfer.
- Maintenance by a TCEQ-registered company, with the responsible provider holding an MP licence and the technician an MP licence or technician registration.
- The whole county except the City of Austin.

**How Aerowise prepares it:**
- The inspection form asks for the pump-tank sludge level in inches and the spray-area condition (Acceptable / Needs attention / Not a spray system) on every Williamson report; a report without them stays a draft.
- The report carries your licence, the technician's registration and your signature, and is held until those are on record.
- The inbox shows the report due 10 days after the test and warns three days before.
- The next visit is booked four months out, or six months with monitoring on record.
- A renewal shows as due 30 days before the old contract ends.
- When you press Send, the report is emailed to ossf@wilcotx.gov from Aerowise, and the filing records when and to whom.

**What you would still do by hand today:**
- Press Send on each report (deliberately not automatic until the county has accepted one).
- Confirm each new site is outside Austin's city limits (the office records that it checked).
- Send **contracts** the same way; they go to the same address when you send them.
- Keep whatever acknowledgment the county gives you, if any; none is published, so Aerowise records only that the email went.

**Drafts not confirmed with the county:**
- *Unconfirmed:* **which inbox providers use.** The homeowner packet (updated August 2026) sends reports to ossfcontracts@wilcotx.gov; the county's pages use ossf@wilcotx.gov. Aerowise sends to ossf@wilcotx.gov until the county says otherwise. This is the first question.
- *Unconfirmed:* 10 or 14 days for the report.
- *Unconfirmed:* renewal 30 days before expiry (the affidavit) versus "within 30 days of expiration" (the brochure).
- *Unconfirmed:* whether the county wants the spray-area line on reports for systems with no spray area.
- *Unconfirmed:* whether the county accepts Aerowise's report layout and an electronic provider signature in place of its own form. (The county publishes an inspection form only in the homeowner packet.)
- Not checked: the chlorine grab sample the county's own form asks for on surface-application systems.

**What the pilot proves:** one real report, prepared here, read against one the county already accepted, sent to the county's inbox by email, and whatever reply comes back recorded. If the county confirms the inbox, the deadline and the layout, the ruleset is marked confirmed and the "press Send" step can stay or go as you prefer.
