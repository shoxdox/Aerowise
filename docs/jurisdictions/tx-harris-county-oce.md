# Harris County Engineering, Wastewater (`tx-harris-county-oce`): draft profile

Status: **draft**, contracts only. Written 2026-10-04.

- source: https://oce.harriscountytx.gov/Divisions/Watershed-Protection/Wastewater/Wastewater-Quality-Permitting (read 2026-10-04): owners of permitted OSSFs must have a maintenance contract; "submit your current Maintenance Contract to wastewater@harriscountytx.gov". No last-updated date shown.
- requirement in effect: **not published** (`requirementEffective.date: null`). The contract duty itself is 30 TAC 285.7(d)(2).
- sources read: 2026-10-04. Aerowise applies the ruleset from that day only because that is when it was written; it is not Harris County's legal effective date. Harris inspections and contracts dated earlier go to review.
- applicability: Harris County, `assignment: "reviewed"`. Cities inside the county may run their own programs; every site needs a reviewed assignment.
- inspection reports: **submission not published** (`inspectionReports.submission: "unknown"`). Inspection filings for Harris sites are held in review and never sent.
- testing and visit schedule (from the 2026-10-04 merge review, **not read by Claude**: the PDF is blocked by robots.txt for the fetch tool): Harris County's testing acknowledgment form, https://oce.harriscountytx.gov/Portals/43/Documents/Publications/OSSFTESTRQ2016.pdf, names Engineering–Permits as the recipient. For the listed treatment systems it requires quarterly visits for residential systems and monthly visits for commercial or other non-single-family systems, plus annual BOD/TSS and other tests. Aerowise's statewide four-month interval is not enough for those systems. A v2 profile has to model those schedules and the tests before Harris inspection reports are enabled, and the submission channel still has to be confirmed.
- contracts: filed by email to the address above. The deadline depends on the kind of contract under 30 TAC 285.7(c), (d)(2) and (d)(3)(C), as set out on the LCRA page. The ruleset's own note still says the timing was read through a summarizer, because published v1 cannot change. The LCRA page records the later reading.
- confirmation: none published.
- not a source: permit applications and installation permitting pages describe new systems, not maintenance report submission.
