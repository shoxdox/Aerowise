Research date: October 6, 2026. Public evidence review; authority confirmation and production acceptance remain pending. PDF page references below are physical pages in the saved file, not embedded court-record pagination.

## C1: The scanned rules are readable and approval is established

Disposition: **public_evidence**. Confidence in source reading: high. Actual authority acceptance: unconfirmed.

The 13-page official scan includes county adoption October 24, 2013 and final TCEQ approval November 26, 2013. Sections 8–9 incorporate TCEQ rules and future amendments. The local additions in §10 concern construction/siting; this order does not add a different recurring maintenance interval, report deadline, report template or lab schedule.

Proposed action: Remove the scan-is-unreadable blocker. Keep permit-specific requirements and later amendments as a separate check; do not turn absence in this order into proof no other requirement exists.

- [Comal County Rules for On Site Sewage Facility](../sources/comal-rules.pdf) — PDF pp. 6–7, 10–13; [official source](https://www.comalcounty.gov/documentcenter/view/2141)

## C2: County role inside five cities has documentary support

Disposition: **partial**. Confidence in source reading: high. Actual authority acceptance: unconfirmed.

The county publishes intergovernmental agreements for Bulverde, Fair Oaks Ranch, Garden Ridge, Schertz and Spring Branch. They describe county OSSF permitting/inspection/enforcement functions. The agreements for multi-county cities limit exclusive county service to the Comal portion. Termination/renewal provisions mean publication alone is not proof of current coverage for every address.

Proposed action: Use these agreements as assignment evidence alongside the actual site permit. Confirm any termination or superseding agreement; never assign solely by city name or county.

## C4: Spray-area obstruction rule is useful to field work

Disposition: **public_evidence**. Confidence in source reading: high. Actual authority acceptance: unconfirmed.

Section 10.9 prohibits items such as picnic tables, play equipment and BBQ pits within the surface application spray area. The order and current saved OSSF Information page do not substantiate the draft assertion that self-maintaining homeowners submit no reports.

Proposed action: Use spray-area condition as a field check. Keep the homeowner no-report exception unconfirmed until a current primary source or county reply supports it.
