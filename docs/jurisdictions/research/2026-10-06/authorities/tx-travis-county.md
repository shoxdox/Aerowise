## T1: Additional report and contract fields are mandatory in the chapter

Disposition: **implementation_gap**. Confidence in source reading: high. Actual authority acceptance: unconfirmed.

Chapter 448 §448.034(e) requires unauthorized alterations, applicable spray-area condition, permit number, printed name/signature, site physical address and provider physical/business addresses, business phone and emergency phone. Contracts have additional fields under (f). The section begins with a single-family applicability statement; commercial applicability should be reviewed separately.

Proposed action: Convert questions about unauthorized alterations and emergency contact into documented template/data gaps for the scoped workflow. Do not infer a typed name meets signature acceptance or that the exact format is approved.

## T2: The 1,000-foot Lake Travis boundary claim is contradicted

Disposition: **conflict**. Confidence in source reading: high. Actual authority acceptance: unconfirmed.

The county non-residential checklist states LCRA review within 2,000 feet of the 691-foot contour. LCRA’s own program describes a 2,000-foot Lake Travis zone. Its order §6 defines the below-691-foot restricted zone and a water-quality zone within 2,000 horizontal feet from the outer restricted-zone boundary in the drainage area. This contradicts a simple 1,000-foot exclusion in the draft.

Proposed action: Correct the research claim and require permit/map-based reviewed authority assignment. Consult LCRA’s jurisdiction map for candidate sites and seek a determination where unclear; do not approximate the contour with a present shoreline distance.
