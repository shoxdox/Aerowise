20.) Consider and take action to appoint Wilma K. Peavy to the BVCOG Board of Directors, with a term beginning October 1, 2024, and ending September 30, 2027.

Department: County Judge

Wilma K. Peavy was recommended by Commissioner Barbara Walker. Ms. Walker passed out a bio of Ms. Peavy.  Charles Tomkins term expires at the end of September.  It is time to have another individual representint the minority citizens of Grimes County on the BVCOG.  Ms. Peavy is here if you have any questions.  She has a wealth of experience and dedication. Retired indvidual as executive director for procurement and disbursements from Prairie View A&M Univiersity and Texas A&M Systems. City of Navasota worked assisting for various needs as the budget analysis and independent contractor to assist until a permanent employee was hired.  She has served as the City Wide Mission 501c3 non profit organization for the Navasota Grimes County Community.  Awarded the Navasota Grimes County Chamber of Commerce 2023 community service award.  Annual events such as youth summits, care packages, Thanksgiving feast providing more than 750 meals, serving at nursing homes and MLK celebrations for the City of Navasota.  Married for 36 years to Mr. Gerald Peavy. 8 children and 23 grandchildren.
