CONCLUSIONS OF LAW,

The Commission has jurisdiction to issue Orders designating local
governmental entities as authorized agents. TEXAS WATER CODE ch. 5
and TEXAS HEALTH & SAFETY CODE ch. 366.

The Commission may delegate uncontested matters to the Executive
Director provided the required notice was given, the applicant agrees to the
action and the application is uncontested. TEXAS WATER CODE § 5.122.

Notice of Montgomery County’s intent to adopt a new Order was properly
provided. TEXAS HEALTH & SAFETY CODE § 366.031 and TEXAS
ADMINISTRATIVE CODE § 285.10.

Montgomery County agreed to the proposed Order in writing.

The proposed Order is uncontested.

The Montgomery County’s proposed Order incorporates the Commission’s
rules on abatement or prevention of pollution and prevention of injury to
the public health; meets the Commission’s minimum requirements for on-
site sewage disposal systems. TEXAS HEALTH & SAFETY CODE §
366.032.
