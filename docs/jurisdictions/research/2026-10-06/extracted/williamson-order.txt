On May 7, 2021, the Executive Director of the Texas Commission on Environmental
Quality ("Commission" or "TCEQ"), considered the application of Williamson County for
an Order pursuant to §366.031, Texas Health and Safety Code (THSC), and 30 Texas
Administrative Code (TAC) §285.10 of the rules of the Commission.

No person has requested a public hearing on the application, therefore the Executive
Director, on behalf of the Commission, is satisfied that Williamson County has satisfied
the requirements of §366.031, THSC. The Commission finds that the Williamson County
Order should be approved.

                                 FINDINGS OF FACT

       1.     Williamson County drafted a proposed Order which regulates on-site
              sewage facilities.

       2.     On January 6, 2021, Williamson County caused notice to be published, in
              a newspaper regularly published and of general circulation, in the
              Williamson County’s area of jurisdiction, of a public meeting to be held on
              January 12, 2021.

       3.     Williamson County held a public meeting to discuss its proposed Order on
              January 12, 2021.

       4.     The Williamson County Order regulating on-site sewage facilities was
              adopted on January 12, 2021.

       5.     A certified copy of the minutes was submitted to the Texas Commission on
              Environmental Quality.

       6.     A certified copy of the Williamson County Order was submitted to the
              Commission.

       7.     The Order is at least equivalent to the standards of the Commission.
                                CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

       1.     The Commission has jurisdiction to issue Orders designating local
              governmental entities as authorized agents. TEXAS WATER CODE ch. 5
              and TEXAS HEALTH & SAFETY CODE ch. 366.

       2.     The Commission may delegate uncontested matters to the Executive
              Director provided the required notice was given, the applicant agrees to the
              action and the application is uncontested. TEXAS WATER CODE § 5.122.

       3.     Notice of Williamson County’s intent to adopt a new Order was properly
              provided. TEXAS HEALTH & SAFETY CODE § 366.031 and TEXAS
              ADMINISTRATIVE CODE § 285.10.

       4.     Williamson County agreed to the proposed Order in writing.

       5.     The proposed Order is uncontested.

       6.     The Williamson County’s proposed Order incorporates the Commission’s
              rules on abatement or prevention of pollution and prevention of injury to
              the public health; meets the Commission’s minimum requirements for on-
              site sewage disposal systems. TEXAS HEALTH & SAFETY CODE §
              366.032.


       NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDERED BY THE TEXAS COMMISSION ON
       ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY THAT:

       1.     Williamson County is hereby authorized to implement its new Order
              regulating on-site sewage facilities.

       2.     Any amendments to the Williamson County Order must be approved by the
              Commission.

       3.     The Office of Chief Clerk of the Commission is directed to forward a copy
              of this Order and the Williamson County’s adopted Order, marked as
              Exhibit “A,” to Williamson County and all other parties and to issue the
              Order and cause it to be recorded in the files of the Commission.
