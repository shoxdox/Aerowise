Inspections of OSSFs. The Designated Representative may conduct inspections of
an OSSF at any time, regardless of when the authorized agent or Designated
Representative conducted the last inspection.

Connection to Organized Disposal Systems. No person may cause or allow the
installation of an OSSF when any part of the OSSF is located within three hundred
feet in horizontal distance (measured on the closest practicable access route) of an
existing Organized Disposal System, with the exception of written denial of service
from the owner or governing body of the Organized Disposal System.
