B. Maintenance Requirements

1. Beginning on the effective date of this Order, in order to provide greater
public health and safety protection, the maintenance for all aerobic
treatment units shall be performed by a TCEQ licensed maintenance
provider or registered maintenance technician unless:

a. The property owner is a TCEQ licensed maintenance provider or
registered maintenance technician or

b. The property owner completes the OSSF Homeowner Aerobic
Treatment & Surface Application System Operations and
Maintenance Class provided by TEEX.

2. The property owner shall submit documentation of completion of the
OSSF Homeowner Aerobic Treatment & Surface Application System
Operations and Maintenance Class to the TCEQ Designated
Representative for Grimes County (see 30 TAC 285.2(17)) (“the DR”
herein) if performing his/her own maintenance as provided above.

3. Notwithstanding the foregoing, should one or more of the following occur,
a property owner performing his/her own maintenance will be required to
contract with a licensed maintenance provider for maintenance of his/her
aerobic treatment unit:

a, The property owner fails to submit the required testing and timely
reporting results to the DR;

b. The property owner falsifies the required documents; or

ec. The DR determines that the owner has violated 30 TAC 285, this
Order, or their permit.
