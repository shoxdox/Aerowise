SECTION 12. COLLECTION OF FEES.

All fees collected for permits and/or inspections shall be made payable to Comal
County, Texas. A fee of $10 will also be collected for each on-site sewage facility permit
to be paid to the credit of the TCEQ Water Resources Management Account as required
by the THSC, Chapter 367.

SECTION 13. APPEALS.

Persons aggrieved by an action or decision of the designated representative may
appeal such action or decision to the Commissioners Court of Comal County, Texas.
