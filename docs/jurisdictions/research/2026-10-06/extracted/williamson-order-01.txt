CODE §366.031 ORDER § QUALITY

On May 7, 2021, the Executive Director of the Texas Commission on Environmental
Quality ("Commission" or "TCEQ"), considered the application of Williamson County for
an Order pursuant to §366.031, Texas Health and Safety Code (THSC), and 30 Texas
Administrative Code (TAC) §285.10 of the rules of the Commission.

No person has requested a public hearing on the application, therefore the Executive
Director, on behalf of the Commission, is satisfied that Williamson County has satisfied
the requirements of §366.031, THSC. The Commission finds that the Williamson County
Order should be approved.

FINDINGS OF FACT
L Williamson County drafted a proposed Order which regulates on-site
sewage facilities.
2. On January 6, 2021, Williamson County caused notice to be published, in

a newspaper regularly published and of general circulation, in the
Williamson County’s area of jurisdiction, of a public meeting to be held on
January 12, 2021.

3. Williamson County held a public meeting to discuss its proposed Order on
January 12, 2021.

4. The Williamson County Order regulating on-site sewage facilities was
adopted on January 12, 2021.

5. A certified copy of the minutes was submitted to the Texas Commission on
Environmental Quality.

6. A certified copy of the Williamson County Order was submitted to the
Commission.

7. The Order is at least equivalent to the standards of the Commission.
