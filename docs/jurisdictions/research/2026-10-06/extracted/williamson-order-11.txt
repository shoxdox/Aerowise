10. A climate controlled structure, which is separated from the main residence,
does not have plumbing and is used for storage, exercise space, as
a workshop, office space, music space, artistic space, animal sheltering or similar
is not considered a part of the single family living space structure for the purpose
of wastewater flow design calculations.

11, License expiration: The license to operate OSSF systems required to meet
secondary treatment shall be valid for two (2) years. At the end of two (2) years, if
the system is receiving the required inspections, is properly operating and a valid
maintenance contract or a homeowner's contract pursuant to Subsection 10.E.6
above is in effect, the license may be renewed upon payment of the renewal fee
in accordance with a fee schedule adopted by the Commissioners Court.

12. A property owner is allowed to have two recreational vehicles or travel trailers on a
lot with an on-site sewage facility that is licensed to operate for a residence. The
recreational vehicles or travel trailers may not be connected to the residential on-site
sewage facility and they may not be occupied as vacation rentals, temporary homes
or domiciles. If a property has more than two recreational vehicles or travel trailers
or the property contains more than two recreational vehicles or travel trailers that are
being used as a vacation homes, a temporary homes or domiciles, then the property
owner must obtain the appropriate OSSF permit.

13. All on-site sewage facility permit applications that include flows from manufactured
homes, recreational vehicles, travel trailers or multifamily developments, must
comply with the infrastructure requirements in the Williamson County Subdivision
Order, the Williamson County Manufactured Home Order and the Emergency
Services Department Regulations.

14, OSSF treatment units or disposal fields may not be placed in the regulated FEMA.
floodway.

SECTION 11. DUTIES AND POWERS.

The OSSF Designated Representative (DR) (30 TAC § 285.2(17)) of Williamson County,
Texas, must be certified by the TCEQ before assuming the duties and responsibilities.

SECTION 12. COLLECTION OF FEES.

All fees collected for permits and/or inspections shall be made payable to Williamson
County, Texas. A fee of $10 will also be collected for each on-site sewage facility permit to be
paid to the credit of the TCEQ Water Resources Management Account as required by the THSC,
Chapter 367.
