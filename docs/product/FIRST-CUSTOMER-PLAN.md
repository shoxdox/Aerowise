# FIRST-CUSTOMER-PLAN.md

## Ideal first customer

- 200–1,000 active maintenance contracts
- 1–5 technicians
- Texas
- uses SAFE/spreadsheets/QuickBooks or dislikes current software
- operates in 1–3 counties
- owner accessible by phone
- willing to share anonymized exports/forms

## Pilot offer

We migrate their data.
We configure their counties.
We run alongside current process for one cycle.
Then cut over.

## What we measure

- office minutes per inspection
- technician minutes per inspection
- renewal completion rate
- reporting errors
- missed deadlines
- duplicate entry count
- payment collection time
- support tickets

## Sales proof

Do not rely on:
"I'd use that."

Require:
- real customer data import
- real inspection use
- real report generation
- real payment
- paid pilot or first invoice
