## Scope and external operating decisions

The branch does not release, merge, change DNS, alter production terms, connect providers, send contacts, charge/refund, file to an authority or erase a real company. Direct email/call is the primary dependable pilot route; saved lead notification receipts distinguish simulated, accepted-by-provider, failed and uncertain states. No real outbound delivery was activated or tested.

The export manifest covers every tenant table through included/derived/excluded dispositions, and refuses an unknown new table until it has a disposition. Raw retained photos and available records/documents are covered; missing objects are named. Invoice PDFs are labeled portable copies without active payment links; immutable stored originals remain unchanged. Export is sequential, so a fixture/quiescent company can reconcile precisely, but a changing company should pause writes for its final export. Operational deletion retains the company tombstone and platform metadata, attempts scoped object cleanup and audits failures; external recipient/provider copies, devices and backups remain separate. Backup 14/35-day values are repository defaults, not newly verified production retention.
